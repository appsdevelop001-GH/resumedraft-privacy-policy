## Overview

ResumeDraft helps users create, preview, export, and share resumes on their device. Resume content is stored locally on the device unless the user chooses to share or export it.

## Data Stored Locally

The app stores resume details, template choices, profile photos cropped inside the app, preferences, and temporary/generated PDF files locally on the device. ResumeDraft does not upload resume content to a server controlled by the app developer.

## Firebase Cloud Messaging

ResumeDraft uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. Firebase may process a notification token and delivery diagnostics. The app may subscribe the device to the `all_users` topic so general app notifications can be delivered.

## Google AdMob

ResumeDraft uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect or receive advertising ID, device information, approximate interaction data, ad performance data, and diagnostics according to Google's policies and the user's device settings.

## Remote Template Downloads

ResumeDraft may download resume template metadata, template packages, and preview images from configured Google Drive or HTTPS manifest URLs. Resume data is not sent to those template URLs. Template downloads are used only to display or render available resume templates.

## PDF Sharing

PDF files are generated locally. A PDF is shared only when the user chooses a share, open, or export action. The destination app or service selected by the user may process the PDF under its own privacy policy.

## Data Deletion

Users can delete saved resumes inside the app. Deleting a resume removes the local resume record and associated app-owned profile photo/PDF files where available. Users can also clear all app data from Android system settings to remove local app data.
